BACKGROUND

Whirlstor Mubonde, commonly known as WHIRLSTOR, was born in 2008 in Mutanda, Kalumbila, Northwestern Province, Zambia.

He grew up in a humble home built on love, faith, and hard work. Raised by a caring family that taught him the value of resilience, gratitude, and community.

From an early age, he showed a strong interest in creativity, leadership, and bringing people together through talent and purpose.

EDUCATION

Mutanda Primary School

FOUNDER OF WHIRL TOGETHER

Whirl Together is a talent discovery and development movement founded by Whirlstor Mubonde.

The mission is to bridge every talented person, regardless of social status, to the right audience. All talent is welcome. Together we rise, together we create, together we succeed.

MUSIC & SONGWRITING

Whirlstor recognizes himself as a talented songwriter and artist. He draws inspiration from personal experience, community stories, and a deep belief in authenticity.

His music focuses on truth, growth, and connection, with the goal of inspiring the next generation to use their voice.
